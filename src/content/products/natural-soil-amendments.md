Not every soil problem is a nutrient problem. Sometimes the structure itself needs attention — compacted clay that sheds water, or sandy ground that drains too fast to support root health. Our Natural Soil Amendments address these foundational issues.

Includes beneficial microorganisms selected for Rogue Valley soil conditions, working alongside the mineral components to rebuild the biological ecosystem that healthy soil requires. Structural improvement combined with biological enrichment — because one without the other only goes so far.
