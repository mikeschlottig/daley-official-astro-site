## 4\. The Blog vs. Product Page Strategy

To balance these effectively, follow the **"Technical vs. Tactical"** rule:

| Content Type | **Products Page (Technical)** | **Blog (Tactical/Advice)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Topic Example** | 20 lbs of 8-3-1 Bat Guano" | "When is the best time to fertilize tomatoes in Oregon?" |
| **Focus** | Specifications, N-P-K ratios, pH levels. | Seasonal timing, pest management, weather tips. |
| **AI Intent** | "Where to buy organic soil with mycorrhiza?" | "How to fix poor drainage in my garden?" |
| **The Hook** | "Buy the best ingredients." | "Learn how to use these ingredients like a pro." |
